== Section Summaries

Quick-scan view of each ECL Core Brief Syntax section in this guide and the keywords it contributes to ECL authoring.

=== Simple Expression Constraints (10)

Keywords of the Simple Expression Constraints section: the hierarchy operators selecting descendants, ancestors, children or parents of a focus concept, and the reference set operators.

=== Refinements (1)

Keywords of the Refinements section: the reverse flag selecting an attribute's values rather than its sources.

=== Cardinality (2)

Keywords of the Cardinality section: the range separator and the unbounded maximum.

=== Conjunction and Disjunction (2)

Keywords of the Conjunction and Disjunction section: the intersection and union of expression constraints or refinements.

=== Exclusion and Not Equals (1)

Keywords of the Exclusion and Not Equals section: the difference of two expression constraints.

=== Description Filters (20)

Keywords of the Description Filters section: filters on a concept's descriptions, the term search types, and the description type and acceptability tokens.

=== Concept Filters (10)

Keywords of the Concept Filters section: the definition status filters and tokens.

=== Member Filters (6)

Keywords of the Member Filters section: filters on the members of a reference set.

=== History Supplements (4)

Keywords of the History Supplements section: the history supplement and its profiles, which add inactive concepts through historical associations.

=== Top and Bottom (2)

Keywords of the Top and Bottom section: the operators selecting the concepts of a set that have no ancestor, or no descendant, within the set.
